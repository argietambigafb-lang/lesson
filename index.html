How to read the diagrams

Strings are shown left to right as low E – A – D – G – B – high e. X means do not play that string; O means play it open. Finger numbers: 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky. Fret the string just behind the fret wire and press only hard enough for a clean note.
